# Double-role shoot card

**Read this on your phone while you shoot. About 20 minutes of shooting.**

You are filming yourself twice so you can appear twice in one shot, talking to
yourself. The trick only works if the camera and the light do not change between the
two takes. That is the whole job: keep everything identical, change only where you
stand.

---

## What you need

- Phone, filming **landscape** (sideways).
- Something to hold the phone **completely still**: a tripod, or propped against
  books/a shelf and taped down. Once it is set, **do not touch it again** until the
  shoot is over.
- A room with **steady light**. Indoors with the blinds shut and the lights on is
  perfect. Avoid: sunlight through a window (clouds change it), anything that flickers.
- Two bits of tape for the floor.

---

## Set up once (5 minutes)

1. **Place the phone.** Landscape, at chest height, far enough back that you can stand
   in the left half of the frame with room above your head.

2. **Set the quality.** iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → **4K at 30 fps**.
   Android: in the camera app's video settings, pick **4K / UHD, 30fps**.

3. **Turn stabilization OFF.** iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → turn
   **Enhanced Stabilisation** off. Also make sure **Action Mode** is off in the camera
   app (the little running-person icon must not be highlighted). Android: camera
   settings → turn off "video stabilisation" / "steady video".
   *Why: stabilisation crops and shifts the picture invisibly between takes, and then
   the two halves will not line up.*

4. **Lock focus and exposure.** Stand where you will perform, then have the camera
   pointed at the scene and **press and hold** on your body on the screen for ~2
   seconds until you see **AE/AF LOCK** at the top. Do not tap the screen again after
   that.
   *Why: if the camera re-focuses or re-brightens between takes, the two halves will
   not match.*

5. **Mark the floor.** Put one piece of tape where you will stand for the LEFT
   performance, one for the RIGHT. Looking at the phone screen, the two spots should
   be clearly apart, each in its own half, **with a gap down the middle** — your two
   selves must never overlap.

---

## Shoot (15 minutes)

Do it in **two recordings**, one per side, so you touch the phone as little as
possible.

### Recording 1 — the LEFT side

1. Press record and **walk out of frame**.
2. Stand still, out of shot, and **count to ten**. This empty stretch is the clean
   plate — the pipeline needs it.
3. Walk to the **left** tape mark and do your performance. Talk, gesture, react to
   where the other you will be. **20–30 seconds.**
4. Walk out, wait ~3 seconds, come back and do it **twice more** (3 performances
   total).
5. Walk out and stop the recording. Touch the phone gently — only the stop button.

### Recording 2 — the RIGHT side

6. Press record, walk out, **count to ten** again.
7. Go to the **right** tape mark. Perform the other half of the conversation, three
   times, same as before. Leave pauses where the other you is talking.
8. Walk out, wait 5 seconds with the frame completely empty, then stop.

**If you want the two of you to read as different people**, change a jacket or add a
hat between recordings 1 and 2. Do not move the phone to do it.

---

## The rules that matter most

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never move or bump the phone | The two halves are combined pixel for pixel |
| AE/AF locked before the first take | Otherwise brightness jumps between your two selves |
| Stabilisation off | It shifts and crops each take differently |
| Stay on your own half, never cross the middle | Keeps the composite simple and clean |
| Ten seconds of empty frame at the start | That is the clean plate |
| Same light for both recordings, minutes apart | Daylight moves; do both back to back |
